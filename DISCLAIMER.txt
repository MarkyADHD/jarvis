WHAT JARVIS CAN DO
JARVIS is a personal AI assistant with real, broad access to your machine:
- It can control your PC: open/close programs and websites, media, volume,
  windows, clipboard, lock, sleep and shut down (it asks first), Spotify,
  Twitch, Discord keybinds and smart lights.
- It can modify its OWN source code when you ask it to, using an AI coding
  model running with real file-write access on your machine.
- It listens to your microphone (hands-free or push-to-talk) to respond to
  voice commands.

A FEW PRACTICAL POINTS
- Back up anything important before installing, same as with any software
  that touches your files.
- JARVIS asks before destructive actions like shutting down, but a misheard
  command is always possible with any voice assistant.
- You're in control: JARVIS only runs when you launch it, and you can
  uninstall it at any time.
