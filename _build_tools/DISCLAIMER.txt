Before you install JARVIS, please actually read this.

WHAT JARVIS CAN DO
JARVIS is a personal AI assistant with real, broad access to your machine:
- It can read, create, and (with your spoken confirmation) delete, move, or
  overwrite files across your C:/E: drives -- not just its own folder.
- It can control your PC: open/close programs, control your mouse and
  keyboard, browse the web, control Spotify and smart lights, and more.
- It can modify its OWN source code when you ask it to, using an AI coding
  model running with real file-write access on your machine.
- It listens to your microphone continuously (or via push-to-talk) to
  respond to voice commands.
- It includes an OPT-IN, BETA autonomous trading module (JarvisTrader)
  that can trade REAL MONEY on a Trading 212 account YOU connect with
  YOUR OWN API keys. Nothing is connected and nothing trades unless you
  deliberately set this up yourself -- it ships inert. If you do connect
  it, you are trading with real money, entirely at your own risk.

A FEW PRACTICAL POINTS
- Back up anything important before installing, same as with any software
  that touches your files.
- JARVIS asks for a spoken "yes" before any delete/overwrite/move action,
  but a misheard command is always possible with any voice assistant.
